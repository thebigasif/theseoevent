Format tips:
- PNG with a transparent background works best on the dark cards.
- SVG also works: use the same name with a .svg extension AND change
  the src in the HTML from .png to .svg (or just tell me and I'll switch).
- Light or colored logos read well on the dark background. Pure-dark
  logos may need a white/transparent version.
