Before you ask

The honest answers.

Is there an SEO conference in Bangladesh?

+

Yes. The SEO Event is an in-person SEO conference in Bangladesh. The first edition, Beyond Algorithm, was held in Dhaka on 22 September 2024. A future edition is in the works.

When and where was The SEO Event held?

+

The SEO Event took place on 22 September 2024 at The Attention Network, 16th floor, Dhaka Trade Centre, Kawran Bazar, Dhaka. It ran for a full day.

Who organizes The SEO Event?

+

The SEO Event is hosted by Asif and brings together Bangladesh's search marketing community, from freelancers to agency owners and in-house marketers.

When is the next SEO Event in Dhaka?

+

The date for the next edition is not set yet. Join the email waitlist on this site and you will get the date, venue, and early-bird access first.

What is the $100 Million SaaS SEO Strategy session?

+

It was the headline talk of 2024: a repeatable playbook for building an SEO engine capable of driving a hundred million in SaaS revenue, covering content clustering, BOFU strategy, and link building.

Who should attend an SEO event in Dhaka?

+

It is built for SEO freelancers landing their first clients, agency owners scaling teams, and in-house marketers chasing revenue. Students and career changers exploring SEO in Bangladesh are welcome too.

Is the SEO Event beginner friendly?

+

Yes. The room runs from first-gig freelancers to agency founders, and sessions are built so you can follow along without ten years of experience.
